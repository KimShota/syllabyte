[00:15] Definition of Potential Outcomes

To quantify causality rigorously, we define potential outcomes as the responses a specific unit would exhibit under hypothetical conditions.

The Fundamental Problem

The individual causal effect is simply τ_i = Y_i(1) - Y_i(0). In practice, we only observe one world for unit i: either treated or untreated.

[02:35] Counterfactual Intuition

Because one state is inherently missing, causal inference becomes a missing data problem. We turn to population averages to bridge what an individual observer cannot see.
